# 6-week Claude training plan: Claude, Claude Code and Claude Design

A **daily** plan to get the most out of Claude. Every day has a short **theory** block and a **practical exercise** you do yourself: in Claude chat, in Claude Code (like this session), in Claude Design, or directly in **Paramur** and **Auralis Natura**.

> **Built for:** a founder and builder who already ships real products with Claude Code and wants to master *all* of the interfaces.
> **Cost:** uses only what's in your Claude **Pro/Max** plan plus free tools. Anything that costs extra is flagged with ⚠️.
> **Accurate as of:** October 2026, checked against Anthropic's official docs and help center. Claude changes fast, so see "Stay current" in [Week 6, Day 42](week-6-claude-design-and-capstone.md).

## What you'll be able to do after 6 weeks
- Explain how LLMs work, where they fail, and how to verify their output (AI fluency).
- Write prompts and prompt chains that get expert-level results on the first or second try.
- Run your businesses from **Projects**, with **memory**, **artifacts**, **Research**, **connectors**, **Docs, Slides** and the **Office add-ins**.
- Understand **MCP**, connect servers, build a small one yourself, and create **Skills**.
- Delegate whole tasks to **Cowork** and **Claude in Chrome**, safely.
- Use **Claude Code** like a senior engineer: lean CLAUDE.md, plan mode, tests-first, reviews, **skills, subagents, hooks, plugins**, **routines** and GitHub automation.
- Produce on-brand **designs, decks and prototypes** with **Claude Design**, and hand them to Claude Code to ship.

## Ground rules
1. **Free first.** Stay within your plan.
   - ⚠️ marks things that cost extra: Fable on Pro, fast mode, ultrareview after its free runs, and API keys.
   - Prefer Sonnet or Haiku and lower effort for simple tasks, to save limits.
2. **Privacy first.**
   - **This repository is public.** Keep client data, prices, credentials and internal project details in your *private* repos or notes.
   - Never paste Auralis client health data, or Paramur partner and financial data, into AI tools unless you've anonymized it or have a clear legal basis.
3. **Human in the loop.** Claude drafts, and you approve anything that's sent, published, paid, merged or deleted.
4. **Verify.** Tests for code, sources for facts, a second look for anything legal, medical or financial.
5. **Every repeated correction becomes a rule** in your profile, a project, CLAUDE.md, a skill or a hook.

## Your AI Playbook
Keep this as a **private** document, e.g. a Claude Doc, a note, or a file in a private repo. Update it every Sunday.

```markdown
# My AI Playbook
## My rules            (privacy, review, compliance, "never do")
## Tool chooser        (which tool for which job, and why)
## Best prompts        (prompt + an example of a great output)
## My assets           (projects, skills, agents, hooks, plugins, routines, artifacts)
## Lessons learned     (mistake → the rule I added)
## Next experiments    (ideas to try next week)
```

## How to use this plan with Claude
- Ask Claude to **quiz you** on any day: *"Quiz me on Week 4, Day 23, with 5 questions."*
- Ask for **more depth**: *"Explain Day 31's hooks with a Paramur-specific example."*
- Ask it to **adapt the plan**: *"I have only 30 minutes today; give me the minimum version of Day 12."*
- Ask it to **schedule** the plan: *"Create calendar blocks for the 42 days in my Google Calendar."* Claude asks for your OK first.
